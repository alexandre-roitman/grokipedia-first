# Privacy Policy

**Grokipedia First** · Last updated: July 25, 2026

Grokipedia First is a browser extension that redirects Wikipedia articles to Grokipedia when available. We designed it to be privacy-first: your browsing history is not collected, sold, or transmitted to us.

## What we access

To provide redirects, the extension needs permission to observe navigation on Wikipedia and Grokipedia, and to check whether a Grokipedia page exists (via a lightweight network request). The extension may also inject a popover on Wikipedia when an article is missing on Grokipedia and you have not chosen silent mode.

## What we store

- **Settings** — Your preferences (redirect on/off, fallback mode, language filter, never-ask-again list, etc.) are stored locally using your browser’s extension storage. If browser sync is enabled, settings may sync through your browser account.
- **Optional usage statistics** — Only if you opt in. Simple counters (e.g. redirect count) are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us or any third party.

## What we do not do

- We do not log or store your browsing history.
- We do not track which articles you read.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
- We do not sell or share personal data.

## Network requests

When you visit a Wikipedia article, the extension may send a `HEAD` request to Grokipedia to check if a matching page exists. That request goes directly to Grokipedia’s servers, not to us. Grokipedia’s own privacy practices apply to those requests.

## Third parties

This extension is not affiliated with Wikipedia, Wikimedia, xAI, or Grokipedia. We do not control third-party websites you visit.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the extension package and in this repository.
